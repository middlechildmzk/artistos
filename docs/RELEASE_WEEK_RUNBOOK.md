# Never Alone Release-Week Runbook

Release: **Middle Child – Never Alone (feat. lowly sunday)**  
Release date: **Friday, July 31, 2026**  
Label: **BVSS FVM**  
Distributor: **DistroKid**  
UPC: **882877618355**

ArtistOS should ship with this campaign populated as the active operating workspace. Dates can be adjusted in the UI, but the release-week structure should exist on first use.

## Friday, July 24: regain control

- Confirm final production master, artwork, lyrics, store metadata, credits, and links.
- Confirm Spotify for Artists pitch and Canvas status.
- Verify HyperFollow/pre-save link and tracking links.
- Review the 21 open campaign tasks and identify real blockers.
- Connect Gmail and Spotify inside ArtistOS.
- Create a release-week shortlist from the playlist/curator database.
- Verify that fan suppressions are enforced before any email workflow.
- Prepare the next three social posts and their assets.

## Saturday, July 25: target and personalize

- Rank playlist, blog, YouTube, radio, DJ, creator, and sync targets by fit, trust, route quality, and timing.
- Select a practical daily outreach batch rather than mass sending.
- Verify submission routes for the highest-priority records.
- Draft personalized pitches using recorded facts only.
- Create follow-up dates and log every submission.
- Prepare one emotional story post and one music-first teaser.

## Sunday, July 26: audience warm-up

- Draft a suppression-safe fan email focused on the story and pre-save.
- Review the fan segment and remove unknown or risky records from the sendable audience.
- Create the Gmail draft, review links and formatting, then send only after explicit confirmation.
- Publish a teaser or behind-the-song post.
- Record engagement and meaningful replies in ArtistOS.

## Monday, July 27: curator outreach wave one

- Contact the highest-fit verified targets.
- Prioritize direct relationships, proven routes, and time-sensitive submission windows.
- Log channel, subject, target, date, pitch version, and follow-up due date.
- Mark failed routes or high-risk services clearly.
- Prepare the Tuesday content asset and copy.

## Tuesday, July 28: content and creator use

- Publish the strongest short-form teaser.
- Contact appropriate creators or channels with clear approved-use language.
- Keep the protected master and creator-safe usage rules separate.
- Do not promise zero claims, stems, Content ID status, or commercial sync rights unless recorded as approved.
- Review outreach responses and update stages/outcomes.

## Wednesday, July 29: follow-up and readiness

- Follow up only where appropriate and due.
- Verify every release-day link, asset, caption, and email draft.
- Prepare release-day posts for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and X.
- Prepare the release-day fan email draft.
- Confirm Spotify artist/release metadata when available.
- Resolve remaining blockers or explicitly defer nonessential work.

## Thursday, July 30: final checks

- Run ArtistOS release-readiness check.
- Confirm stores/links that can be verified without making unsupported claims.
- Review scheduled content, drafts, assets, and CTAs.
- Confirm all outbound recipient lists remain suppression-safe.
- Prepare a simple release-day sequence with exact order and copy.
- Avoid unnecessary redesign or data migration on the final day.

## Friday, July 31: release day

ArtistOS Today should present a focused release-day mode:

1. Confirm live release links.
2. Update the canonical smart link if needed.
3. Publish the primary release announcement.
4. Send the reviewed fan email with explicit confirmation.
5. Notify priority contacts and existing relationships.
6. Publish short-form/video content.
7. Log meaningful replies, placements, posts, and problems.
8. Capture first-day metrics available from verified sources.
9. Celebrate completion and surface only critical remaining work.

## Post-release queue

- Follow up with appropriate targets.
- Log placements and coverage with evidence links.
- Thank supporters and creators.
- Continue content without repeating the same post.
- Review fan replies and relationship opportunities.
- Capture verified performance data over time.
- Produce a campaign retrospective: what worked, what failed, costs, outcomes, and next actions.

## Release-readiness score

ArtistOS may show a readiness score, but it must be explainable. Suggested dimensions:

- distribution and metadata
- links and tracking
- artwork/assets
- editorial/platform setup
- fan email readiness
- content readiness
- curator/industry outreach readiness
- rights/use clarity
- integrations health
- unresolved blockers

Never show 100% merely because fields are filled. Critical blocked items must cap the score and explain why.
