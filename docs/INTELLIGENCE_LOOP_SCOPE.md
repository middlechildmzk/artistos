# ArtistOS connected intelligence loop

This slice connects the existing canonical ArtistOS domains without launching a second application or an open marketplace.

## Product graph

Release → Link → Campaign → Target → Submission → Deliverable → Proof → Outcome → Music Intelligence → Artist Brain

## Included

- Campaign creation through the audited capability runtime.
- Searchable, workspace-scoped Network Intelligence.
- Campaign target assignment and outreach.
- Submission receipts created from recorded outreach.
- Deliverable tracking by campaign target.
- Evidence records preserving release, campaign, target, source, verification, confidence, and contradiction lineage.
- ArtistOS Proof workspace for the evidence and outcome ledger.
- Music Intelligence trends, release comparisons, campaign attribution, and attention signals from source-visible metric snapshots.

## Explicitly deferred

- Automated outbound messaging or submission.
- Guaranteed placement or stream claims.
- Open marketplace payments.
- Autonomous external actions.
- Third-party analytics ingestion without reviewed credentials, terms, provenance, and rate-limit controls.
- Competitive intelligence connectors beyond manually sourced and attributed snapshots.

All business writes remain authenticated, workspace-scoped, role-gated, idempotent, and audited through the existing capability runtime.
