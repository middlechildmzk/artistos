## Purpose

Connect the first complete ArtistOS operating loop across Campaign Intelligence, Network Intelligence, Proof, and Music Intelligence.

## What this branch adds

- Searchable and filterable private Network Intelligence.
- Campaign creation through the existing audited capability runtime.
- Submission receipts generated from recorded outreach.
- Campaign deliverable tracking.
- Evidence records with release, campaign, target, verification, confidence, and contradiction lineage.
- ArtistOS Proof as a unified evidence and outcome ledger.
- Music Intelligence trend detection, release comparison, campaign attribution, and attention signals.
- Dashboard navigation that presents the modules as one ArtistOS ecosystem.

## Safety

- No production schema or production data changes in this branch.
- No service-role secret is exposed to the browser.
- No automated outreach, paid placement, guaranteed growth, or autonomous external action.
- Business writes remain workspace-scoped, role-gated, idempotent, and audited.

## Verification required

- TypeScript, unit tests, and production build.
- Authenticated owner, viewer, and outsider regression after the stacked Links PRs merge.
- Disposable-database testing of campaign creation, target assignment, outreach receipt, deliverable creation, outcome evidence, and Proof visibility.
