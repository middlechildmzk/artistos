# MC Bible: ArtistOS Product Source of Truth

Updated: 2026-07-26

## Company thesis

ArtistOS is an artist and music-network intelligence platform that deliberately builds a trusted ecosystem, helps creators make and release better music, manages promotion and relationships, and learns from every campaign outcome.

## Three engines

1. Inbound: education, StackBuilder, templates, comparisons, case studies, and free tools.
2. Outbound: private Network Intelligence for discovery, identity resolution, evidence, enrichment, qualification, outreach, invitations, and profile claims.
3. Core product: Artist Graph, Release Workspace, Creator Studio, Campaign Intelligence, Audience CRM, relationships, evidence, and outcomes.

## Product modules

### Artist Graph

Persistent identity, aliases, catalog, sound, influences, goals, visual identity, collaborators, audience, platform profiles, history, and approved memories.

### Release Workspace

Release facts, audio, artwork, lyrics, metadata, rights-lite, contributors, distribution, deadlines, tasks, campaign assets, readiness, outcomes, and retrospective.

### Creator Studio

Creator Music Prompts becomes structured artist-aware tooling for music prompts, songwriting, positioning, visual direction, release copy, mix/master review, and AI release-risk review.

### Campaign Intelligence

CuratorFit and the music CRM become explainable opportunity matching, trust and risk evidence, submissions, outreach, follow-ups, placements, coverage, and relationships.

### Network Intelligence

Private operating system derived from SourcingOS principles. Discover, resolve, verify, enrich, qualify, contact, follow up, invite, claim, activate, and retain entities across the music ecosystem.

### Growth engine

Free tools and educational content solve one high-intent problem, then allow users to save and continue inside ArtistOS.

## Golden path

`content/free tool -> save -> account -> artist profile -> release -> readiness -> Creator Studio -> campaign -> outreach -> outcome -> retrospective -> next release`

## Trust contract

Every material claim should preserve source, retrieval date, freshness, evidence class, confidence, contradiction state, reviewer status, and permitted use. Verified facts must remain distinct from supported inference, weak signal, unknown, stale, and conflicting evidence.

## Non-negotiables

- No new isolated feature.
- No public launch without workspace isolation.
- No guaranteed streams or placements.
- No AI output silently promoted to fact.
- No private CRM notes exposed through claimed public profiles.
- No sending outreach without explicit user action and suppression checks.
- No unreleased music in public storage.
